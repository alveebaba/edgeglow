EdgeGlow 0.1.1 — Windows x64 / Govee G1 H6609

One-time experimental release. No ongoing maintenance or support.

1. Enable LAN Control in the Govee Home phone app. PC and strip must share a LAN. Exit Govee Desktop.
2. Extract this entire ZIP to a permanent folder. Double-click EdgeGlow.exe; it starts paused. Keep the EXE, DLL and config together.
3. Allow local-network access if Windows Firewall prompts. EdgeGlow uses the first H6609 it discovers. Find strip retries discovery.
4. Choose Display. Leave Picture area on Auto. Tune lighting: click a zone, check its physical position, adjust Rotate zones/Reverse strip and Apply settings. Tests clear after eight seconds.
5. Resume sync. For automatic background use, check Start with Windows and Start syncing when opened. Closing the window hides to tray; Exit EdgeGlow stops it.

Defaults:18 zones (6 top,3 right,6 bottom,3 left),24 updates/s,150 ms smoothness,65% brightness,20% edge depth,125% color strength,Ignore dark background ON.

Automatic screen standby: while syncing, Windows display-off, lock/sleep or selected-display disconnection requests lights off and stops capture. Ten continuous seconds of failed/missing capture also requests off. A healthy still picture stays active; brief errors hold the last colors. Lighting returns after the blockers clear and a fresh image arrives. Manual Pause stays paused. A physical monitor button may be invisible to Windows if its video connection remains active. Network/suspend timing can prevent delivery; test off/wake on your PC.

Supported: H6609 only; Windows10/11 x64,.NET Framework4.8,D3D11 GPU. Tested physically on one Windows11 PC and one strip. Firmware and mounting can vary. This build is unsigned. DRM video and some exclusive-fullscreen games may block capture; try borderless fullscreen. HDR and edge colors are approximate. Rare-blink stability remains unconfirmed after the timing fix.

Settings/logs:%LOCALAPPDATA%\EdgeGlow. No screen images are saved or uploaded.
To remove:uncheck Start with Windows,Exit EdgeGlow,delete this folder; optionally delete local settings/logs.
